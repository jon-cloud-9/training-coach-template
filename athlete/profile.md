# Athlete Profile

Campaign-independent. This survives when a campaign is archived — it is what the next
goal starts from. Update it when a number is re-tested, equipment changes, or a
durable pattern is established. Do not put race-specific targets or week-by-week state
here; those belong in the campaign's `summary.md`.

## Discipline profile

_Your background and strengths in swim, bike and run, and which is the weakest. Filled in during setup._

## Confirmed baselines

_Thresholds and benchmarks (threshold pace, CSS, FTP or HR zones), each with the date it was set. Filled in during setup._

## Equipment and data availability

_What you train with: watch, HR strap, power meter, pool access, and anything that limits the data. Filled in during setup._

## Data reliability — known failure modes

_Any HR, power or sleep data you should not trust, and why. Filled in during setup._

## Health and injury

_Injury history, current niggles, and the signals that mean a session stops. Filled in during setup._

## Behavioural patterns

_How you tend to train: habits, over- or under-doing, what derails you. Filled in during setup._

## Fuelling

_A short pointer to your fuelling protocol and anything about food or digestion that matters. Filled in during setup._
